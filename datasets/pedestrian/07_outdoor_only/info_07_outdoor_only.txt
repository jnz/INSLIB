07_outdoor_only -- 22 Aug 2026, Spain

Handheld, outdoors throughout but deliberately in awkward places: along the
pool, along the house wall (with the unit moving in the hand), up the stairs, a
lap around the courtyard, up the outside stairs to the terrace, back down,
along the house again and back to the start at the pool. Open sky the whole
time, but often close to the building and under trees and palms. Start and end
are the same point to about 5 cm, with a standstill at each.

Heading on the straight legs is 310 deg true (measured with a phone compass).
Fast turns at the corners.

The counterpart to 06: same hardware and the same corrections, without the
indoor stretch, so the two isolate what the building does.

ref_ardupilot.csv is ArduPilot EKF3 fed the same measurements through the same
calibration, i.e. a second estimator rather than a ground truth. Position
errors scored against it are the difference between two filters.

imu.csv is block averaged from the original 783 Hz to 98 Hz; the sensor is
internally filtered, so the full rate carried no additional information.

Sensors:
 - ICM-45686 TDK InvenSense IMU
 - LIS3MDL magnetometer
 - BMP581 Bosch barometer
 - u-blox X20P (ArduSimple board), L1/L2/L5/L patch antenna,
   u-blox Point Perfect Flex (SPARTN) corrections
 - GNSS antenna lever arm to the IMU, body FRD: [0.02, 0.28, 0.01] m
 - IMU and magnetometer calibration taken at 34 degC
