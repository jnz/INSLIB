05_outdoor_to_indoor_no_spartn -- 22 Aug 2026, Spain

Handheld, walked three times: along the pool, up the stairs, along the house,
put down on a marked spot on the ground at the front door, a lap through the
house, put down on the same spot on the way out, back down the stairs and along
the pool to the start. Start and end are the same point to about 5 cm.

Heading on the straight legs is 310 deg true (measured with a phone compass).
Fast turns at the corners.

The receiver ran STANDALONE, with a hard reset before the start and no
correction data at all. Same route and the same hardware as the SPARTN runs, so
the pair isolates what the corrections are worth: the receiver's own loop
closure over this route is about 1.2 m here against 0.01 m with SPARTN, and its
reported horizontal accuracy is 0.78 m against 0.03 m. 147 epochs were dropped
below a 3D fix.

ref_ardupilot.csv is ArduPilot EKF3 fed the same measurements through the same
calibration, i.e. a second estimator rather than a ground truth. Position
errors scored against it are the difference between two filters.

imu.csv is block averaged from the original 786 Hz to 98 Hz; the sensor is
internally filtered, so the full rate carried no additional information.

Sensors:
 - ICM-45686 TDK InvenSense IMU
 - LIS3MDL magnetometer
 - BMP581 Bosch barometer
 - u-blox X20P (ArduSimple board), L1/L2/L5/L patch antenna,
   no corrections (standalone, hard reset before the start)
 - GNSS antenna lever arm to the IMU, body FRD: [0.02, 0.28, 0.01] m
 - IMU and magnetometer calibration taken at 34 degC
