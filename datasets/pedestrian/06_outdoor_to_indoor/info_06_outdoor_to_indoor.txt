06_outdoor_to_indoor -- 22 Aug 2026, Spain

Handheld, walked three times: along the pool, up the stairs, along the house,
put down on a marked spot on the ground at the front door, through the house
including one flight of stairs up and down, put down on the same spot on the
way out, back down the stairs and along the pool to the start. Start and end
are the same point to about 5 cm. Standstills: start, before and after each of
the three house visits, end -- nowhere else.

Heading on the straight legs is 310 deg true (measured with a phone compass).
Fast turns at the corners.

The receiver keeps producing 3D fixes throughout the house; the longest gap in
the whole run is 1.0 s. Indoors its reported velocity accuracy degrades from
about 0.11 m/s to 0.32 m/s (per axis, median), which separates inside from
outside far better than the reported position accuracy does.

ref_ardupilot.csv is ArduPilot EKF3 fed the same measurements through the same
calibration, i.e. a second estimator rather than a ground truth. Position
errors scored against it are the difference between two filters.

imu.csv is block averaged from the original 763 Hz to 95 Hz; the sensor is
internally filtered, so the full rate carried no additional information (the
standstill noise is unchanged by the averaging).

Sensors:
 - ICM-45686 TDK InvenSense IMU
 - LIS3MDL magnetometer
 - BMP581 Bosch barometer
 - u-blox X20P (ArduSimple board), L1/L2/L5/L patch antenna,
   u-blox Point Perfect Flex (SPARTN) corrections
 - GNSS antenna lever arm to the IMU, body FRD: [0.02, 0.28, 0.01] m
 - IMU and magnetometer calibration taken at 34 degC
